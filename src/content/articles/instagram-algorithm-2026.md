Instagram par reach sirf followers ki wajah se nahi milti. Platform alag-alag signals dekhkar decide karta hai ki kisi post ya Reel ko kis audience tak pahunchaya jaye.

## Sabse important signals

- **Watch time:** Log content ko kitni der dekh rahe hain.
- **Shares:** Content kisi aur ko bhejne layak hai ya nahi.
- **Saves:** Content baad mein useful lag raha hai ya nahi.
- **Originality:** Reposted ya low-effort content ke comparison mein original work ko priority mil sakti hai.
- **Audience response:** Comments, profile visits aur follows jaise signals context provide karte hain.

## Practical strategy

Ek clear topic choose karo, first few seconds mein strong hook do, aur video ko unnecessary filler se mat bharo. Sirf hashtags par depend karna sustainable growth strategy nahi hai.

## Bottom line

Algorithm ko beat karne se zyada useful hai audience ko samajhna. Jo content log dekhna, save karna aur share karna chahte hain, wahi long-term mein stronger signal create karta hai.
